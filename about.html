About Us

Our Story

A Christ-centred fellowship equipping individuals and families through the Word, worship, relationships, community and service.

Our Story

Kristo Fellowship began with a simple conviction: that God's love transforms lives and His Kingdom advances through ordinary people who choose to follow Jesus wholeheartedly.

Our fellowship is built on the foundation of biblical truth, vibrant worship, and authentic community. We believe that every person is created with purpose and designed to impact their world for Christ.

From humble beginnings to a thriving multi-generational community, God has been faithful. Today, we gather as families, individuals, and friends who share one passion — knowing Christ and making Him known.

Our approach to fellowship combines traditional biblical values with contemporary expression, creating a space where both young and old can encounter God and grow together.

What We Believe

The Bible

We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible Word of God and the final authority for faith and practice.

Jesus Christ

We believe in Jesus Christ as the Son of God, our Saviour and Lord, who died for our sins and rose again.

Salvation

We believe salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ alone, not by works.

The Holy Spirit

We believe in the present ministry of the Holy Spirit who empowers believers for godly living and service.

The Church

We believe in the Church as the body of Christ, called to worship, fellowship, discipleship, and mission.

Family

We believe God designed the family as the foundational unit of society and the church.
