Our Pillars

Seven Pillars of Fellowship

The foundations that shape who we are and how we live out our faith together.

Our seven pillars represent the core areas of fellowship life. Each pillar has specific ministries and focus areas designed to help you grow and serve.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Family

Strengthening families through biblical principles, relationships and intergenerational fellowship.

Ministries:

  • Vines — Children's ministry
  • Youth — Teen and young adult fellowship
  • Couples — Married and courting couples
📖

Word

Biblical teaching, discipleship, Bible study and spiritual growth.

Focus Areas:

  • Sunday sermons and teaching
  • Small group Bible studies
  • Discipleship programmes
  • Theological training
🎵

Worship

Building a lifestyle of worship, prayer and intimacy with God.

Expressions:

  • Corporate worship services
  • Prayer meetings
  • Personal devotion resources
  • Worship teams and music ministry
🤲

Care

Supporting individuals and families through counselling, compassion, prayer and practical support.

Support Areas:

  • Pastoral counselling
  • Prayer ministry
  • Compassion outreach
  • Practical help for those in need
🏘️

Community

Building meaningful relationships through fellowship, shared experiences and community house fellowships (Hooks).

Expressions:

  • Home fellowships (Hooks)
  • Group discussions
  • Local gatherings and events
  • Fellowship meals and activities
🌍

Missions

Taking the Gospel beyond traditional church settings through evangelism, digital ministry, outreach and missions.

Outreach:

  • Evangelism programmes
  • Digital ministry and online outreach
  • Community service projects
  • Mission partnerships
💼

Marketplace

Equipping believers to live out their faith, influence workplaces, conduct business ethically and create meaningful impact.

Focus:

  • Faith at work resources
  • Ethical business principles
