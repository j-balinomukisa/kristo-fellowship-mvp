We're glad you're here

Kristo Fellowship is a welcoming community where you can encounter Christ, grow in faith, and serve alongside others. Whether you're exploring faith for the first time or looking for a church home, you belong here.

Next Steps:

  • Attend a Sunday service (10:00 AM)
  • Introduce yourself at the welcome desk
  • Join a Community Hook (home fellowship)
  • Connect with a ministry that matches your gifts

Confidential Pastoral Care

Our pastoral team offers counselling support for individuals and families navigating life's challenges — from grief and loss to relationship struggles, spiritual questions, and decision-making.

All counselling is provided from a biblical perspective and maintained in strict confidence.
