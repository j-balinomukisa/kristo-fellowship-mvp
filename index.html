Our Vision & Mission

Professing God's Love • Advancing His Kingdom • Empowering Change Agents

❤️

Vision

A fellowship where every person encounters Christ, is discipled in the Word, and is equipped to impact their world for the Kingdom of God.

📖

Mission

To build Christ-centred families and communities through biblical teaching, worship, relationships, and service that transforms lives and advances His Kingdom.

🤝

Values

Christ-centred • Welcoming • Family-oriented • Biblical • Contemporary • Community-driven • Spiritually empowering

Our Seven Pillars

The foundations of our fellowship life

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Family

Strengthening families through biblical principles, relationships and intergenerational fellowship.

📖

Word

Biblical teaching, discipleship, Bible study and spiritual growth.

🎵

Worship

Building a lifestyle of worship, prayer and intimacy with God.

🤲

Care

Supporting individuals and families through counselling, compassion, prayer and practical support.

🏘️

Community

Building meaningful relationships through fellowship, shared experiences and community house fellowships (Hooks).

🌍

Missions

Taking the Gospel beyond traditional church settings through evangelism, digital ministry, outreach and missions.

💼

Marketplace

Equipping believers to live out their faith, influence workplaces, conduct business ethically and create meaningful impact.
