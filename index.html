About Kristo Fellowship

Our Story, Heart & Doctrine

Rooted in the timeless truth of Scripture, committed to relational community, and dedicated to raising salt and light in our generation.

The Journey

Our Story

Kristo Fellowship was birthed from a deep spiritual conviction: that the Church of Jesus Christ must be both the pillar of uncompromising biblical doctrine and a warm, loving household where weary hearts find healing, families are strengthened, and believers are equipped for marketplace impact.

Moving beyond traditional Sunday-only routines, our vision has always been to weave Christ into everyday spaces—around dinner tables in house fellowships (Hooks), in boardroom decisions, and through intergenerational family discipleship.

Our Positioning
A Christ-Centred Fellowship

Equipping individuals and families through the Word, worship, relationships, community and service.

Biblical Welcoming Family-Oriented Empowering

Our Vision

To be a vibrant, Christ-centred movement of believers actively professing God’s love, advancing His Kingdom, and raising generational change agents who transform families, workplaces, and communities.

Our Mission

To nurture disciples through deep biblical teaching, cultivate passionate worship and prayer altars, provide safe and confidential pastoral care, strengthen godly marriages and youth, and commission believers into the marketplace.

Doctrinal Tenets

What We Believe

Our uncompromised biblical foundation

1. The Holy Scriptures

We believe the 66 books of the Holy Bible are the verbally inspired, inerrant, and authoritative Word of God, our supreme rule for faith, practice, and doctrine.

2. The Triune God

We believe in one God, eternally existent in three distinct co-equal persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

3. The Person & Work of Jesus Christ

We believe in His virgin birth, sinless life, substitutionary atoning death on the cross, bodily resurrection, ascension to the right hand of the Father, and personal return.

4. Salvation by Grace

Man is justified solely by God's grace through faith in Jesus Christ, regenerated by the Holy Spirit unto eternal life.

5. The Covenant of Family

God instituted the family as the cornerstone of society. We uphold biblical marriage, covenant faithfulness, and the intentional discipleship of children.

6. The Great Commission & Marketplace

Every believer is commissioned to make disciples and demonstrate Christ's ethical standards in government, business, education, and arts.

Kristo Fellowship Privacy Notice

1. Strict Pastoral Confidentiality

In accordance with Section 9 of the Kristo Fellowship Product Requirements Document, prayer and counselling submissions remain strictly private and will never appear publicly under any circumstances. Access is restricted exclusively to specifically vetted and authorized pastoral intercessors.

2. Minimum Data Collection

We collect only the information necessary to minister to you. When requesting prayer, you are free to submit completely anonymously without providing your name or telephone number.

3. Third-Party Integrations

For MVP video streaming, we embed video players via YouTube. We do not sell, rent, or distribute member or visitor personal data to commercial third parties.

4. Contact & Inquiries

For inquiries regarding data privacy or to request the deletion of your pastoral record, contact our data steward at privacy@kristofellowship.org.
